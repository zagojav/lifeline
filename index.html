Lifeline

Backup & Restore for Shopify
English Português Español Français Deutsch

Lifeline is a Shopify app that makes automatic backups of your store's products and collections, so you can restore them in a couple of clicks when something goes wrong.

What it does

  • Backs up products, variants, images and metafields.
  • Backs up collections, including the exact product order.
  • Keeps track of which sales channels each item was published on.
  • Restores any backed-up item, and lets you undo a restore.
  • Never touches customers, orders or payment data.

No tracking

This site and the app don't use cookies or any kind of visitor tracking. The only data the app stores is described in the privacy policy below.
