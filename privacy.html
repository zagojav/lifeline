Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Lifeline makes backups of your Shopify store's products and lets you restore a product when something goes wrong. This page explains, in plain words, what data we keep and what we do with it.

What we store

  • Products: title, description, handle, vendor, type, status, tags, SEO, options and variants (price, compare-at price, SKU, barcode and the other variant settings).
  • Product images: a copy of each image and its alt text. An identical image used in several backups is stored only once.
  • Product metafields.
  • Collections: title, handle, description, SEO, theme template, metafields, the rules of automated collections, a copy of the collection image and, for manual collections, the list of products in the exact order they are shown.
  • Sales channels: for each product and collection, which sales channels (Online Store, Shop, POS, markets) it was published on, just the channel names. This lets a restored item become visible again.
  • Restore history: what was restored, when, and the name of the store staff member who did it. We don't store their email.
  • Technical store data: the store address (e.g. mystore.myshopify.com) and the access keys Shopify provides so the app can work.

What we DON'T store

  • Customer data (name, email, phone, address).
  • Orders.
  • Payments or card data.

The app asks Shopify for product permission (write_products) and sales channel permissions (read_publications and write_publications), used only to read and redo which channels a product or collection is published on. It has no access to customers or orders.

Where we store it

On servers in Brazil. Each store's data is kept separate, and only the app can access it.

How long we keep it

  • Backups: 30 days (this may change with your plan; the current period is shown on the app's home page). The most recent backup is always kept while the app is installed.
  • Images: deleted once no stored backup uses that image anymore.
  • Undoing a restore: available for the same period as backups.
  • Restore history: twice the backup period (60 days by default), so you can see what was done even after undo expires.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall the app. Tasks stop right away. Backups are kept for 48 hours in case you reinstall. After that, Shopify notifies us and we delete everything for your store: backups, images, history and settings.
  • If you reinstall the app within those 48 hours, your backups are still there.
  • After deletion, we keep only a record that it happened (the date and an irreversible code derived from the store address), to prove the deletion was done. This record can't be used to recover any data.
  • To delete earlier, email zagolabs.support@proton.me.

Customer requests

If a customer of your store asks for their data or asks for it to be deleted, Shopify notifies us. Since we don't store customer data, there's nothing to send or delete, and we log the request without any customer data.
