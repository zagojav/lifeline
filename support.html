Frequently asked questions

What does Lifeline store?

Product and collection data (title, description, variants, images, metafields, sales channels) and your restore history. Full list in the privacy policy. Customer, order and payment data are never stored.

For how long are backups kept?

30 days by default (the period can change with your plan; the current one is shown on the app's home page). The most recent backup is always kept while the app is installed.

How do I restore a product or collection?

Open the app from the Shopify admin, pick the backup you want from the item's history, and confirm the restore. The item is updated in your store right away.

How do I undo a restore?

Right after a restore, the app shows an undo option for the same period as your backup retention (so 30 days by default). Undoing brings the item back to how it was right before the restore.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Backup tasks stop immediately. Your data is kept for 48 hours in case you reinstall; after that, it's deleted permanently. See the privacy policy for details.
